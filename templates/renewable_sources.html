Renewable Energy Sources Help
About Renewable Energy Monitoring

This dashboard provides real-time monitoring of renewable energy sources across India's power grid. It shows both installed capacity and current generation for different renewable technologies.

Renewable Energy Sources
  • Solar: Photovoltaic and concentrated solar power plants.
  • Wind: Onshore and offshore wind turbines.
  • Hydro: Hydroelectric power plants (typically those under 25 MW capacity are classified as renewable).
  • Biomass: Power plants using organic material as fuel, including agricultural waste and dedicated crops.
  • Others: Includes geothermal, tidal, wave energy, and other emerging renewable technologies.
Key Metrics
  • Capacity Utilization: The percentage of installed capacity currently generating power.
  • Contribution to Grid: The percentage of total grid power coming from renewable sources.
  • CO₂ Avoided: Estimated carbon emissions avoided by using renewable instead of fossil fuels.
Factors Affecting Generation

Renewable energy generation can vary significantly based on:

  • Weather conditions (sunshine, wind speed)
  • Seasonal variations
  • Time of day
  • Maintenance schedules
  • Grid constraints and curtailment
